[Scene below.] Two original adult characters with noticeably different facial shapes and features, facing each other naturally in conversation. Expressive friendly faces, realistic proportions within a stylized animated-film aesthetic, warm daylight and detailed but uncluttered interior. Both faces clearly visible. No text, logos, lettering, speech bubbles or watermarks.
